Every sponsorship team starts with spreadsheets. A shared Google Sheet with partner names, deliverable lists, and status columns. It works fine when you have five partners and twenty obligations. Then the season starts, and everything breaks.

## The problems compound

**Version control disappears.** Three people update the same sheet. Someone overwrites a status. Someone else adds rows in the wrong section. By mid-season, nobody trusts the data.

**Proof gets scattered.** Game-day photos land in someone's camera roll, a shared Google Drive folder, a Slack thread, and an email attachment — all for the same deliverable. When it's time to build a recap, you spend hours hunting down the right images.

**Reporting is manual.** Building a recap report means copying data from the spreadsheet into a slide deck, formatting tables, inserting photos, and trying to make it look professional. For every single partner. After every single event.

**Nothing is connected.** The spreadsheet doesn't know about the photos. The photos don't know about the deliverables. The recap doesn't update when a status changes. Every piece of the workflow lives in a different tool.

## What a purpose-built tool changes

When your tracking, proof collection, and reporting all live in one platform:

- **Statuses update automatically** — upload proof, and the deliverable moves to "proved" without manual intervention
- **Proof is attached to deliverables** — every photo, video, and document is linked to the obligation it fulfills
- **Recaps generate in seconds** — fulfillment stats, proof galleries, and category breakdowns populate automatically from your data
- **Everyone sees the same truth** — team members, managers, and partners all work from the same real-time view

## The shift is worth it

Moving from spreadsheets to a dedicated platform feels like a big change. But the time you save on every event — hours of manual reporting, proof hunting, and status reconciliation — adds up fast. For teams managing 20+ partners across a full season, the difference is measured in weeks, not hours.

Seira was built specifically for this workflow. If your spreadsheets are holding you back, it might be time to try something better.
