If you work in sponsorship — whether for a sports team, venue, or festival — you know the pain of fulfillment tracking. Dozens of partner obligations spread across spreadsheets, email threads, and shared drives. Due dates that slip. Proof photos buried in camera rolls. Recap reports cobbled together at the last minute in PowerPoint.

We built Seira to fix all of that.

## What Seira does

Seira is a purpose-built platform for sponsorship fulfillment. It gives your team one place to:

- **Track deliverables** organized by category — in-venue, digital, signage, hospitality, talent, and content
- **Collect proof of performance** by uploading photos, videos, and documents directly to each deliverable
- **Generate branded recap reports** with fulfillment stats, proof galleries, and category breakdowns — shareable via link or PDF

No more chasing down proof photos after the event. No more wondering which deliverables are overdue. No more spending hours formatting recap decks.

## Who it's for

Seira is designed for the teams who sell, deliver, and report on sponsorship commitments:

- **Sports teams** managing season-long sponsor packages across 80+ home games
- **Venues and arenas** coordinating naming rights, concourse activations, and hospitality deliverables
- **Festivals and live events** fulfilling multi-day sponsor commitments in real time

## Get started

You can create a free account and start tracking deliverables in minutes. Import obligations from a template or build your list from scratch. Invite your team with granular roles and permissions.

We're excited to help sponsorship teams work smarter. Welcome to Seira.
