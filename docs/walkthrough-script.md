# Seira Product Walkthrough — Video Script

**Runtime:** 3–5 minutes | **Format:** Screen recording + voiceover

---

## 1. Onboarding (0:00–0:25)

**Screen:** `/onboarding`

Name your workspace, pick your org type — sports team, festival, conference — and Seira tailors your templates to match.

Create your first event. Add your first sponsor. Pick a deliverable template to pre-fill items, or start blank.

Three steps, under a minute, and you land on your event page ready to go.

`Step 1: workspace name + org type → Step 2: event name, venue, date → Step 3: partner name + template → Finish`

---

## 2. Seasons (0:25–0:40)

**Screen:** `/seasons` → New season

Group events into a season or series to track combined performance. Set a date range, and every event you assign rolls up into aggregate stats, partner performance, and asset utilization.

`+ New season → name + dates → Create`

---

## 3. Events (0:40–1:00)

**Screen:** `/events`

Every game, concert, or activation lives here. The Next Up strip highlights your soonest event with its progress bar and overdue count. Sort by date, name, or completion. Each row shows partners, deliverable progress, and alerts at a glance.

`Show list → point out Next Up strip → click into event`

---

## 4. Partners + Templates (1:00–1:30)

**Screen:** Event detail → Add Partner

Inside an event, add sponsors and manage their deliverables. Pick a template to pre-fill items — or type a deal summary and hit Suggest Deliverables. The AI reads your deal text and generates matching line items automatically.

`Add Partner → enter deal info → pick template OR click Suggest Deliverables → deliverables populate → Add Partner`

---

## 5. Batch Deliverables (1:30–1:50)

**Screen:** Season detail → Batch Create

For seasons, create deliverables across every event at once. Pick a template, set date rules — "event day + 7 days" — and Seira resolves dates relative to each event automatically. Preview the total: 8 events times 6 deliverables, 48 items created in one click.

`Batch Create → pick template → set date rules → preview → Create`

---

## 6. Game Day Tracking (1:50–2:10)

**Screen:** Partner detail page

On game day, track fulfillment in real time. Status chips show exactly where things stand — not started, in progress, done, proved. The proof collection bar tracks how many deliverables have verified documentation. Update statuses as activations happen; the dashboard reflects changes instantly.

`Show status chips → update status dropdown → progress bar updates`

---

## 7. Proof Upload — Mobile (2:10–2:40)

**Screen:** Deliverable detail, mobile viewport

Proof upload is built for phones. Your team snaps a photo on-site, taps Add Proof, and uploads in seconds. AI validation fires automatically — it checks that the image actually shows sponsor branding and flags mismatches before they reach your recap.

Tap a thumbnail to view full-screen with the validation result: "Sponsor logo visible on LED board — high confidence."

`Tap Add proof → select photo → upload animates → validation badge appears → tap thumbnail for lightbox`

---

## 8. AI Risk Alerts (2:40–3:00)

**Screen:** `/dashboard`

The dashboard surfaces what needs attention. Overdue deliverables, pending approvals, and items missing proof are prioritized by urgency — highest-risk items first. The renewal pipeline shows which partners are up for re-signing and their current fulfillment score, so nothing slips through the cracks.

`Show stats row → scroll to Attention card → point out overdue/approval/proof dots → show Upcoming Renewals`

---

## 9. Recap Generation (3:00–3:20)

**Screen:** Partner detail → New Recap

When an event wraps, generate a recap report. Deliverables and proof populate automatically. Hit Generate with AI and the system writes a cover note from your actual data — completion rates, proof highlights, category breakdown. Edit if you want, or publish as-is.

`+ New Recap → click Generate with AI → cover note appears → review`

---

## 10. Share (3:20–3:40)

**Screen:** Recap page → Publish

Publish to create a shareable link. Send it to your sponsor — they see a polished report with proof photos, fulfillment stats, and your branding. No login required. Share via link or email with one click.

`Publish → copy link → show public recap view with hero, fulfillment ring, deliverables grid`

---

## 11. Talent + Usage Rights (3:40–4:00)

**Screen:** `/talent` → deliverable detail

Manage players, artists, influencers, and speakers in one directory. Link talent to deliverables and set usage rights — scope, territory, and duration. When rights are about to expire, they surface on the dashboard automatically so your legal team stays ahead.

`Show talent directory → link talent to deliverable → show usage rights fields → cut to dashboard expiring rights`

---

## 12. Team Management (4:00–4:15)

**Screen:** `/settings/team`

Invite your team with role-based access. Owners and admins manage everything. Contributors create content and upload proof. Viewers get read-only access. Change roles anytime with a clear permission diff showing exactly what changes.

`Show member list → Invite → select role → show Change Role permission diff`

---

## 13. Analytics + Close (4:15–4:35)

**Screen:** `/dashboard` filtered → `/partners`

Filter the dashboard by event, season, partner, or category to slice your data any way you need. The partner directory rolls up every sponsor across all your events — health scores, deal values, completion rates, renewal dates. Select up to three partners to compare side-by-side.

That's Seira — from onboarding to recap, everything your partnerships team needs in one place.

`Apply dashboard filters → navigate to partner directory → select two partners → Compare`

---

**Estimated runtime:** 4:35
